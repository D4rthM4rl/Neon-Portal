# Goal
Beat each level as fast as you can by standing in front of the green triangle (touch the triangle while on the ground). You can spawn portals on walls, floors, and ceilings if the portal creation line as indicated by your portal creation line being solid with a green check icon at the end as opposed to dashed with a red x at the end.

# Controls
Computer: Use the arrows or WASD to move and jump, and the mouse to spawn portals on the side of walls and floors. Remove portals by tapping R and restart the level by holding R. Press escape to bring up the pause menu.
Mobile: Use the arrows on the left part of the screen to move. Use the Up arrow on the right part of the screen to jump. Press the small x button to remove your portals and press the cycle icon below it to switch the next portal creation. Use the smaller circle in the bigger circle on the right as a joystick which slows down time while you aim where to spawn your next portal.
