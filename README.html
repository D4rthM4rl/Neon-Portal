Goal

Beat each level as fast as you can by standing in front of the green triangle (touch the triangle while on the ground). You can spawn portals on walls, floors, and ceilings if the portal creation line as indicated by your portal creation line being solid with a green check icon at the end as opposed to dashed with a red x at the end.

Controls

Computer: Use the arrows or WASD to move and jump, and the mouse to spawn portals on the side of walls and floors. Remove portals by tapping R and restart the level by holding R. Press escape to bring up the pause menu. Mobile: Use the arrows on the left part of the screen to move. Use the Up arrow on the right part of the screen to jump. Press the small x button to remove your portals and press the cycle icon below it to switch the next portal creation. Use the smaller circle in the bigger circle on the right as a joystick which slows down time while you aim where to spawn your next portal.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2025

Overview

Neon Portal values your privacy. This policy explains what information the game collects, how it’s used, and your rights as a player.

Information Collected

When you play Neon Portal, the following data may be collected automatically by Unity’s services: Analytics Data: gameplay events, device type, region (approximate), and anonymous identifiers generated by Unity. This information helps me understand how players interact with the game and improve performance and design. Leaderboard Data: a username and score for displaying on leaderboards. A random username is created for new players, which can be edited in the options menu.

How Your Data Is Used

To analyze gameplay trends and performance using Unity Analytics. To display leaderboard rankings using Unity Leaderboards. To maintain and improve the game’s functionality and player experience.

Data Linked to You

No personal information (such as real names, email addresses, or contact details) is collected. The identifiers used (like device IDs or usernames) are anonymous and cannot be used to identify you personally.

Third-Party Services

This app uses services provided by Unity Technologies: Unity Analytics Unity Gaming Services Leaderboards These services collect and process data in accordance with Unity’s privacy policies.
